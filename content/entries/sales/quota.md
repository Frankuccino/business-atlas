Quota is the individual revenue target that, summed across the team, makes the company plan. Coverage is how much pipeline must exist to make that quota realistic rather than hopeful.

The two are a system. Quota without coverage is a wish; coverage without quota is activity with no target. Setting them together is the core of sales management.

## Worked example

A seller closes 20% of qualified opportunities and has a 60-day cycle. Quota is $120,000 for the quarter:

    Needed closed value      = 120,000
    Needed opportunities     = 120,000 / 20% = 600,000 in pipeline
    Coverage                  = 600,000 / 120,000 = 5.0x

At a 20% win rate the seller needs five times quota in pipeline. Now raise the win rate to 40% with better qualification:

    Needed opportunities     = 120,000 / 40% = 300,000
    Coverage                  = 2.5x

The same quota became half as demanding — not by working harder, but by qualifying better. That is why coverage ratios should be derived from actual win rates, not copied from a benchmark.

## Watch for

- **Attainment should cluster near 60-70%.** Everyone hitting quota means it is too low; almost nobody hitting it means it is demotivating and wrong.
- **Quota is a commitment, not a ceiling.** Capping earnings turns high performers into the people who stop trying.
- **Coverage differs by segment.** A new-logo motion needs more coverage than an expansion motion. Blending them produces a number that fits neither.
