Competitive advantage is a position that lets you earn more than your rivals for the same effort. The word "sustain" is the whole concept: a lead is an advantage you have not yet converted.

The distinction between better and different is the practical core. Being better is a race anyone can join, and it ends in a price war. Being different changes the comparison the customer makes, so the race is not run at all.

## Worked example

Two accountants serve small businesses in the same town:

    A. "We are accurate and affordable."  — every accountant claims this. No comparison is possible, so price decides.
    B. "We specialise in restaurants, and we know your margin structure."  — the restaurant owner compares B to no one.

B is not better. B is different, and the difference is worth more to one specific buyer. That is an advantage, because it cannot be copied by a generalist without giving up their own generalist customers.

## Watch for

- **Advantage needs a customer.** A difference nobody values is a cost, not an advantage.
- **Advantage erodes.** Competitors copy, markets shift, and technology changes the basis of competition. Treat it as a depreciating asset.
- **Saying it is not having it.** "We have great people" and "we move fast" are not advantages — they are claims every competitor makes.
