The anchor text for this project. Its value is not that it is comprehensive —
it is that it is a single map of how a business works, in plain language, with
the vocabulary defined on first use.

Use it as the skeleton for the Foundations, Finance, and Unit Economics layers,
and treat it as one source among several rather than the authority. Where it
disagrees with a specialist text (pricing, negotiation, venture capital), the
specialist wins.

## What it contributes

- The value equation: value created minus value captured.
- The four ways a business can make money, and why the model matters more than
  the margin.
- A working vocabulary for finance, marketing, sales, and operations.

## What it does not

- It does not cover fundraising, cap tables, or term sheets. Those belong to the
  Capital domain, which has its own sources.
- It is not a playbook for a specific decision. Use it for vocabulary, then go
  to the playbooks for the checklist.
